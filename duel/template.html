How it learned

Each dot on the axis is a saved brain you can pick above. Early on they wander and wave their blades at nothing. Later they close in, swing on purpose and start blocking each other.

What's going on

Same idea as the viral clip: virtual flies trained with PPO, a standard trial-and-error learning algorithm, by fighting copies of themselves. No connectome is involved; the controller is a small neural network (16 inputs, two layers of 64, 3 outputs: walk, turn, swing).

Simpler than the clip: the fight runs in 2D on the arena floor with a simplified fly body, and was trained right here in a few minutes of CPU.
