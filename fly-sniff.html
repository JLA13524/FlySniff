    Activity features drive six antennal-lobe glomeruli; from there it's the real wiring of the fly's smell pathway, 38 projection neurons, 4,064 Kenyon cells and 97 MBONs with their synapse counts, from the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome (Janelia, Google Research and collaborators, via neuPrint, CC BY 4.0). The page opens on a demo run with made-up training. Upload .fit or .fit.gz files (from your watch, or the activities folder of a Strava export) and the fly smells them right here in your browser. Files never leave your browser. python -m flysniff does the same offline, plus kudos as dopamine; drop its results.json here to see it. Not science.
